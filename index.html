How a Night goes

  1. The host picks a theme and shares a 5-letter code.
  2. Friends join from their phones — joining is always free.
  3. Everyone takes a turn submitting one YouTube music video that fits.
  4. Videos play in sync for the whole room. Chat and fling emoji while they do.
  5. Don't fancy what's up next? The room can veto it before it plays.
  6. Blind vote at the end. Most votes wins the Night.

Questions people actually ask

Do we need accounts or wifi?

No accounts, ever. Phones talk straight to each other over WiFi and Bluetooth — nothing about your Night leaves the room. You only need internet to stream the videos themselves.

How many people can play?

Anyone in the room with an iPhone and the host's code. Everyone picks one video, so most Nights run three to six people.

Is it free?

Joining is always free, and so are Wildcard, Guilty Pleasures and One-Hit Wonders plus your whole Night history. One optional purchase unlocks the rest — once, not monthly.

Can we watch on the TV?

Yes — AirPlay to an Apple TV and the big screen becomes the stage while phones stay the remotes.

Privacy · Support

Music Video Night is not affiliated with YouTube or Google. Videos play through YouTube's official embedded player.
