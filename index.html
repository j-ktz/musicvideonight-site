How a Night goes

  1. The host picks a theme and shares a 5-letter code.
  2. Friends join from their phones — joining is always free.
  3. Everyone takes a turn submitting one YouTube music video that fits.
  4. Videos play in sync for the whole room. Chat and fling emoji while they do.
  5. Don't fancy what's up next? The room can veto it before it plays.
  6. Blind vote at the end. Most votes wins the Night.

Why it's different

📺

Put it on the TV

AirPlay to an Apple TV and the big screen becomes the stage — video, reactions, scores. Phones stay the remote.

🔌

No server, no accounts

Everyone's in the same room, so phones talk to each other over WiFi and Bluetooth. Nothing to sign up for.

🎭

Themes with a personality

80s Divas, Rap Night, Sad Bangers, Guilty Pleasures — or make up your own and name it yourself.

👑

Bragging rights that last

Every Night is saved, and the Hall of Fame tracks whose taste actually wins across all of them.
